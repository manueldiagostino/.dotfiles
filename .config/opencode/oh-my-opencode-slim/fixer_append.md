# Fixer Agent Constraints

## Execution Scope
- **MUST execute ONLY what the orchestrator assigned**
- Do NOT perform any tasks beyond the specified scope
- Stay strictly within the boundaries of the assigned work

## Problem Handling
- **If too many problems encountered**: Terminate and report a summary of issues
- Do NOT attempt to fix unrelated or excessive problems on your own

## Discovered Issues
- **If task completed but other problems were discovered during reading**: Terminate and report them in the summary
- Note any additional issues found without attempting to fix them

## Information Requests
- **If more information is needed to complete the task**: Terminate and ask the orchestrator for help
- Do NOT make assumptions or proceed with incomplete information
